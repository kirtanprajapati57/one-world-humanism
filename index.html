THE ONE-WORLD HUMANIST MANIFESTO
ONE HUMANITY. ONE WORLD. ONE FUTURE.

Preamble

We believe that before we are citizens of nations, members of communities, followers of traditions, or people of different backgrounds, we are human beings.

Humanity has divided itself by borders, caste, race, religion, wealth, nationality, language and countless other identities. These identities may shape our cultures and experiences, but they should never determine the value or dignity of a human life.

We envision a future in which humanity gradually moves beyond unnecessary divisions and builds a civilization founded upon human dignity, individual freedom, peace, knowledge, cooperation and progress.

We do not claim that a perfect world can be created overnight. We seek to create a better world—step by step, generation by generation.

I. ONE HUMANITY

There is only one human family.

No human being is inherently superior or inferior because of their birth, nationality, caste, wealth, religion, language, gender or social background.

Human dignity is universal.

II. FREEDOM OF THE INDIVIDUAL

Every person should have the freedom to shape their own life. A free human being should have the opportunity to choose their education, career, ideas, ambitions and way of living.

But freedom carries responsibility. My freedom cannot become another person's oppression.

III. A WORLD WITHOUT UNNECESSARY BORDERS

Borders are historical and political creations; humanity itself has no natural borders.

We envision a future in which people can increasingly move, study, work, collaborate and live across the world without being unnecessarily restricted by nationality.

IV. PEACE ABOVE WAR

War should never be humanity's first answer.

Nations should increasingly resolve disputes through diplomacy, international law, negotiation, institutions and peaceful cooperation.

V. EQUALITY OF HUMAN DIGNITY

Equality does not necessarily mean that every human being will have the same abilities, interests, achievements or possessions. It means that every human being deserves equal fundamental dignity and rights.

VI. THE END OF EXTREME POVERTY

A civilization capable of reaching the stars should not accept a world where people are unable to meet their most basic needs.

Every human being should have reasonable access to food, clean water, shelter, education, essential healthcare and safety.

VII. EDUCATION AS A HUMAN RIGHT

Education should not exist merely to produce workers. Its deeper purpose is to produce thinking human beings.

Education should teach people how to think critically, question assumptions, understand evidence, communicate, create, discover and continuously learn.

VIII. SCIENCE, REASON AND KNOWLEDGE

Humanity must remain willing to question itself. We believe in evidence over unsupported claims, reason over dogma, curiosity over ignorance and discovery over fear.

IX. HUMANITY AND THE PLANET

Earth is the common home of humanity. Economic and technological development must therefore consider the generations that come after us.

X. HUMAN ADVANCEMENT

The purpose of civilization should be more than survival. Humanity should seek to understand more, create more, discover more and become capable of more.

THE FINAL DECLARATION

We do not promise paradise.

We do not claim that human conflict can disappear completely.

We believe something simpler:

Humanity can become better.
